## Definition
- Expressions will be a combination of [[Sources of Values|Values]], [[Variable|Variables]], [[Operator|Operators]], and [[Method Call|Method Calls]].
- Expressions evaluate to/return a single [[Sources of Values|Value]].
- There are three types of expressions in Java:
	- Expressions that produce a value. `(pi*radius)+2`
	- Expressions that assign a value. `number = 35`
	- Expressions that modify (reassign) a value. `x +=5` or `i++`
	- Expressions that call a method. `myMethod()`
