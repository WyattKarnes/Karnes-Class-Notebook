## Game Pitch
- What kind of game?
- Why is yours special?
	- Who are the characters? If its a custom player character, what can be customized? How might that affect gameplay?
	- What is the setting?
	- What devices will this run on?
- Roadmap given the [B/G Calendar](https://wasatch.edu/whs/wp-content/uploads/sites/5/2023/09/2023-24-WHS-BlackGold.pdf). 
> [!warning] Take your time on this. Actually plan it out given how much time you have total. You can adjust this plan as you go, but make sure to spread your work across all available time.

- Delivery schedule including- 
	- Base mechanics completion
	- Story/Campaign/Levels Writing or design completion
	- Prototyping completion
	- Visual Design Completion	
	- Final Development
	- Playtesting
	- QA/Bugfixes
	- Release
- Consider "cost" in time both in and out of class FOR EACH DELIVERABLE
	- time in class
	- time out of class
	- combined time
	- Total combined project time
