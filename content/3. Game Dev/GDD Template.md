## Description
- Write a brief description of your game. Like an outline, or a sales pitch. Imagine you are writing the back of a game box, or an online store description.
### Rules
- Give detailed "rules" that govern how your game works. 
- This includes things like player stats, abilities, enemy types, and just game systems in general. 
### Level/Room/World Design
- Draw up some levels. You can do this digitally or on paper. 
### Script
- This is the stuff that a player might hear from a narrator, or that characters might say to the player and vice/versa. Its literally a script.
### Game Mechanics
- This includes the challenges presented to the player as well as the actions the player can take to overcome them.
### Goals
- What does it take to win or lose the game?
### Platform(s)
- What platform or platforms would you like to release to?
