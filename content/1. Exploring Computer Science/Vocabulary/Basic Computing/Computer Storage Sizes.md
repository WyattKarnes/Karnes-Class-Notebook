## Foundational
### Bit
- A single 0 or 1
### Nibble
- 4 bits
### Byte
- 8 bits

---
## Consumer Sizes

### Kilobyte
- 1000 Bytes
### Megabyte
- 1000 KB
- 1 million Bytes
### Gigabyte
- 1000 MB
- 1 billion Bytes
### Terabyte
- 1000 GB
- 1 trillion Bytes

- 1000 EB
- 1 sextillion Bytes
